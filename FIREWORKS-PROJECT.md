# FireWorks Project — Tidyline Cleaning Co.

## Template family and demo safety

Recurring Property Services. Primary demo industry: residential and commercial cleaning. Tidyline Cleaning Co. is fictional. All names, phone numbers, emails, reviews, credentials, hours, coverage, availability, services, task lists, prices, and claims are sample content. Forms validate locally only. They do not send or store inquiries, upload documents, confirm availability or bookings, create recurring schedules, or take payments.

## Intake and CTA

Primary CTA: Request a Quote.

Fields: property path, approximate property size, bedrooms, bathrooms, commercial area/facility type, cleaning type (routine/deep/move-in/move-out), frequency, preferred date, ZIP code, broad notes, name, phone, email, and explicit demo acknowledgment.
