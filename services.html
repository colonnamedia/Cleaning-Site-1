Boundaries first

What is not assumed.

A real proposal may add specialty work after review, but the standard template does not imply services the client has not confirmed.

Safety exclusions

  • Biohazards or bodily fluids
  • Mold, pest, or trauma remediation
  • Unknown chemicals or unsafe materials

Access exclusions

  • High exterior windows
  • Unsafe ladders or roof access
  • Moving unapproved heavy items

Specialty exclusions

  • Carpet extraction unless added
  • Construction debris unless scoped
  • Laundry or dishes unless selected
